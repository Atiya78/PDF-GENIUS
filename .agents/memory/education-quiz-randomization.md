---
name: Quiz answer positions
description: The owner's requirement for unpredictable MCQ answer positions and stable scoring and exports.
---
Use independent random option order for each multiple-choice question. Do not impose an A/B/C/D rotation or a fixed quota of each answer letter; consecutive identical answer letters are legitimate random outcomes.

**Why:** The owner reported generated quizzes always placing the correct answer at A, and explicitly rejected a predictable repeating sequence as the replacement.

**How to apply:** Randomize once at generation completion and retain that same order in practice, exports, and saved results. Preserve the correct answer's text identity. Leave true/false and non-choice formats unchanged, and avoid explanations or options that depend on choice letters or positions.
