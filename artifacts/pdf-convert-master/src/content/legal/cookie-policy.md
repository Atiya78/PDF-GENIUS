## 1. Summary

The PDF Genius website **does not set any first-party cookies**. It uses your browser's local storage for things the site needs to work. The only non-essential technology is the **Google Ads tag**, which is **not loaded until you choose "Accept" or enable advertising cookies** in the consent banner. You can change your choice at any time using **Cookie settings** in the footer.

## 2. Strictly necessary storage (always on)

These keep the site working and cannot be switched off. They stay in your browser and are not used for tracking.

| Name | Type | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `auth_token` | Local storage | Keeps you signed in | Until you sign out or delete it |
| `pdfgenius-consent` | Local storage | Remembers your cookie choices | 12 months, then we ask again |
| `chunk-reloaded` | Session storage | Prevents a reload loop after a site update | Until the tab is closed |

## 3. Functional storage (always on, on-device only)

| Name | Type | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `search-history-*` | Local storage | Your recent tool searches, shown back to you | Until you clear it |
| `search-analytics` | Local storage | On-device counts of searches to rank suggestions; never sent to us | Until you clear it |

## 4. Advertising cookies (only with your consent)

If you accept advertising cookies, the site loads Google's tag (`gtag.js`, account AW-18278560130) to measure whether our Google Ads lead to completed downloads. Google may then set these cookies:

| Name | Set by | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `_gcl_au` | Google Ads (on {{domain}}) | Stores ad-click information to attribute conversions | 90 days |
| `_gcl_aw`, `_gcl_gs` | Google Ads (on {{domain}}) | Stores the ad click identifier when you arrive from an ad | 90 days |
| Google cookies such as `IDE`, `NID`, `AID` | Google (on google.com / doubleclick.net) | Ad measurement and personalisation under Google's policies | Up to 13 months |

Google's use of this data is described in [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). Before you consent, Google Consent Mode is set to "denied" and the tag is not loaded, so no Google Ads cookies are set.

## 5. Third parties during sign-in and checkout

- **Google Sign-In:** when you choose "Continue with Google", Google's sign-in window uses Google's own cookies on Google's domains to authenticate you.
- **Paddle (Education checkout):** when you open an Education checkout, Paddle's checkout script runs and may use cookies and storage needed for payment and fraud prevention, under [Paddle's privacy notice](https://www.paddle.com/legal/privacy).
- **Dodo Payments (PDF Tools checkout):** checkout happens on Dodo Payments' own page, under Dodo's policies.

These are necessary to complete the action you requested.

## 6. Android app

The Android app does not use cookies or third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. It stores your sign-in token and app settings on your device.

## 7. Managing your choices

- Use **Cookie settings** in the site footer to accept, reject or customise at any time. Withdrawing consent stops the tag from loading on future page views; you can also delete existing cookies in your browser settings.
- If your browser sends a **Global Privacy Control** signal, we treat it as a rejection of advertising cookies.
- Blocking local storage in your browser will sign you out and prevent the site from remembering your choices.
