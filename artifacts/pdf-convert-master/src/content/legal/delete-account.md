## Delete in the Android app

1. Open PDF Genius and sign in.
2. Go to the **Settings** tab.
3. Tap **Delete account**, type DELETE to confirm, and tap **Delete permanently**.

## Delete on the website

1. Sign in on [{{domain}}]({{website}}).
2. Open your [Profile](/dashboard/profile).
3. In **Delete account**, type DELETE and select **Delete my account** — or use the button on this page while signed in.

## Request deletion by email

If you cannot sign in, email [{{privacyEmail}}](mailto:{{privacyEmail}}) from the address registered on your account with the subject "Delete my account". We may ask you to confirm you own the account, and we complete the request within 30 days (usually much sooner).

## Before you delete

- **Cancel any active subscription first.** The site will not delete an account while a PDF Tools or Education Zone subscription is still active, so you are never charged for an account that no longer exists. See the [Cancellation Policy](/cancellation-policy). Android in-app subscriptions must be cancelled in Google Play.
- **Download anything you want to keep.** Deletion cannot be undone.
- **Unused credits are lost.** Credits in either wallet are removed and cannot be refunded after deletion, except where the law requires.

## What is deleted

Deletion is **immediate and permanent**:

| Data | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Account (email, name, password hash, profile photo) | Deleted |
| Conversion history and stored output files | Deleted, including files in storage |
| PDF Tools and Education credits, plan and wallet records | Deleted |
| Education history, usage records and API keys | Deleted |
| Notifications and pending verification codes | Deleted |

## What may be kept

- **Payment records** held by Dodo Payments, Paddle or Google Play, which they must keep for tax and accounting law under their own privacy policies.
- **Support emails** you sent us, kept only as long as needed to handle your request.
- **Short-lived server logs and encrypted database backups**, which are overwritten on their normal schedule and are not used to restore deleted accounts.
