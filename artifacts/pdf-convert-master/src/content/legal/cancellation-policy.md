## 1. How subscriptions work

PDF Tools plans (Pro, Business) and Education Zone plans (Basic, Pro, Max) are billed monthly or yearly in US dollars and **renew automatically** at the end of each billing period until you cancel. Each product has its own subscription; cancelling one does not cancel the other.

## 2. Cancel a PDF Tools plan (Dodo Payments)

1. Sign in on [{{domain}}]({{website}}).
2. Open [Pricing](/pricing) (shown as "Manage Plans" when you are signed in).
3. Select **Manage Billing**. This opens the Dodo Payments customer portal.
4. Choose cancel for your subscription and confirm.

You can also cancel using the link in your Dodo Payments receipt email, or ask us at [{{supportEmail}}](mailto:{{supportEmail}}).

## 3. Cancel an Education Zone plan (Paddle)

1. Sign in and open [Education pricing](/education/pricing).
2. Select **Manage Education subscription**. This opens the Paddle customer portal.
3. Choose cancel and confirm.

You can also cancel from the link in any Paddle receipt email or at [paddle.net](https://paddle.net).

## 4. Cancel an Android in-app subscription (Google Play)

Open the Google Play Store app → your profile icon → **Payments & subscriptions** → **Subscriptions** → PDF Genius → **Cancel subscription**. Uninstalling the app does not cancel it.

## 5. What happens after you cancel

- No further renewal charges are made.
- Your plan stays active until the end of the current paid period, then your account moves to the free tier.
- PDF Tools credits you bought stay in your PDF Tools wallet. Education credits you bought stay in your Education wallet until they expire 12 months after purchase; monthly Education plan credits stop being added.
- Cancelling does not by itself refund the current period. To ask for a refund, see the [Refund Policy](/refund-policy).

## 6. Changing plans

You can switch plans from the same billing portal. The Merchant of Record calculates any proration or charge and shows it before you confirm.

## 7. Cancellation by us

We may cancel a subscription if payment repeatedly fails or for a serious breach of the [Terms](/terms). If we cancel without cause, we refund the unused part of the current period.

## 8. Deleting your account

You cannot delete an account while a paid subscription is still active. Cancel it first, wait for it to end, then [delete your account](/delete-account) — or email [{{privacyEmail}}](mailto:{{privacyEmail}}) and we will help.
