## 1. Purpose

This policy is part of our [Terms of Service](/terms). It keeps PDF Genius safe, lawful and available for everyone. It applies to the website, the Android app and the API.

## 2. Illegal and harmful content

Do not upload, process, generate or share content that:

- is illegal where you are or in the United Kingdom;
- sexually exploits or endangers children (we report such content to the relevant authorities);
- promotes terrorism, violent extremism or credible threats of violence;
- harasses, defames or threatens others, or reveals someone's private information without consent;
- is used for fraud, phishing, forgery of official documents or identity theft.

## 3. Intellectual property

Only process files you own or have permission to use. Do not use the tools to remove copy protection, watermarks or passwords from documents you are not authorised to unlock, or to infringe copyright, trademarks or other rights. See the [Copyright / DMCA Policy](/dmca).

## 4. Security and malware

Do not upload viruses, malware or files designed to exploit software. Do not probe, scan or test the Service for vulnerabilities without our written permission, bypass authentication, plan limits or rate limits, or interfere with other users.

## 5. Abuse of the Service and API

- No automated scraping, crawling or bulk use of the web tools; use the [API](/docs) instead.
- No reselling, sublicensing or white-labelling raw access to the Service or API without a written agreement.
- No sharing of API keys or accounts, or creating multiple accounts to get around free limits.
- No sending of traffic intended to overload the Service.

## 6. AI features

Do not use the AI features to generate unlawful, hateful or sexual content, to impersonate real people, to create misleading content presented as genuine, or to try to extract the underlying prompts or models. Do not rely on AI output for decisions with legal, medical or financial consequences without independent expert review. See the [AI Usage Policy](/ai-policy).

## 7. Academic integrity

Do not use the Education Zone to cheat in exams or assessments, to submit generated work as your own where your institution forbids it, or to obtain unauthorised help during a live test. Use it to study, revise and prepare.

## 8. Enforcement

We may remove content, limit features, suspend or close accounts that break this policy, and report illegal activity to authorities. Where reasonable we will tell you why and give you a chance to respond. To report abuse, email [{{supportEmail}}](mailto:{{supportEmail}}).
