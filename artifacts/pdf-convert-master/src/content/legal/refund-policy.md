## 1. Summary

> You can request a refund within **{{refundDays}} days** of a purchase. Refunds are issued by the Merchant of Record that took your payment — **Dodo Payments** for PDF Tools, **Paddle** for the Education Zone, **Google Play** for Android in-app purchases — back to your original payment method. Credits from a refunded purchase are removed from your wallet.

PDF Tools and Education Zone are separate products with separate wallets, so each purchase is refunded by its own provider under the section below that applies to it.

## 2. PDF Tools purchases (Dodo Payments)

Applies to Pro and Business subscriptions and PDF Tools credit top-ups bought on the website. Dodo Payments is the Merchant of Record and issues the refund under this policy and the [Dodo Payments terms]({{dodoBuyerTerms}}).

- **Subscriptions — first payment:** you can request a full refund within {{refundDays}} days of your first payment for a plan.
- **Subscriptions — renewals:** you can request a refund within {{refundDays}} days of a renewal charge if you have not used paid features since that renewal.
- **Credit top-ups:** you can request a refund within {{refundDays}} days of purchase if the credits from that purchase are mostly unused. If some were used, we refund the unused portion.
- When a subscription is refunded, the plan ends and your account returns to Free. When credits are refunded, the refunded credits are removed from your PDF Tools wallet.

## 3. Education Zone purchases (Paddle)

Applies to Basic, Pro and Max Education subscriptions and Education credit top-ups. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record and reseller for these orders, and refunds follow this policy and the [Paddle Checkout Buyer Terms]({{paddleBuyerTerms}}).

- **Subscriptions — first payment:** you can request a full refund within {{refundDays}} days of your first payment.
- **Subscriptions — renewals:** you can request a refund within {{refundDays}} days of a renewal charge if you have not used that period's credits.
- **Credit top-ups:** you can request a refund within {{refundDays}} days of purchase if the credits from that purchase are mostly unused.
- When Paddle issues a full or partial refund, credits granted by that purchase are reduced in proportion to the amount refunded. Credits you already spent cannot be restored to a refunded purchase.
- You can also request a refund from Paddle directly through the link in your Paddle receipt email or at [paddle.net](https://paddle.net).

## 4. Android in-app purchases (Google Play)

Subscriptions and credit packs bought inside the Android app are sold by Google Play. Refunds are handled under Google Play's refund policy: request one from your Google Play order history, or contact us and we will help where Google allows.

## 5. EU and UK consumers — 14-day right of withdrawal

If you are a consumer in the EU, EEA or UK, you have a legal right to withdraw from a purchase within 14 days without giving a reason. For digital services and digital content, this right can end once delivery begins if you expressly asked for immediate access and acknowledged that you would lose the right — for example, by using credits right away. If you withdraw from a subscription after you have started using it, you may be charged for the portion of the service already provided. This does not reduce any other rights you have under this policy.

## 6. How to request a refund

1. Email [{{supportEmail}}](mailto:{{supportEmail}}) or use the [contact form](/contact) with the topic "Billing".
2. Include the email address on your account, the order or receipt number from the Dodo Payments or Paddle receipt, and the reason for the request.
3. We reply {{supportResponse}}. Approved refunds are sent by the Merchant of Record to the original payment method and usually appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

## 7. Cancellation is not a refund

Cancelling a subscription stops the next renewal; it does not refund the current period on its own. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for. See the [Cancellation Policy](/cancellation-policy).

## 8. Chargebacks

Please contact us before disputing a charge with your bank — most issues can be fixed quickly. If a chargeback is filed, the Merchant of Record handles it, and the related subscription or credits may be suspended until it is resolved. Fraudulent chargebacks may lead to account termination.

## 9. When refunds are not given

Except where the law requires otherwise, we do not refund requests made more than {{refundDays}} days after purchase, credits that have already been used, Education credits that have expired, or purchases connected to fraud, abuse or a breach of our [Terms](/terms).

## 10. Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits your rights under consumer protection law, including rights for services that are faulty or not as described. Where the law or the Merchant of Record's own buyer terms give you more favourable rights, those rights apply.
